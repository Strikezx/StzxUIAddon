# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- Info: hover the levelling readout for the exact numbers: XP (current / needed and %), XP to the next level, rested XP (and how much of a level it is), finished quests waiting to be handed in (how many, their XP, and where they'd take you), and this session's time, XP gained, XP per hour, time to level, last gain and average gain (about how many more like it to level).
- Skins: windows that lay Blizzard's border straight onto themselves (DBM's options window) lose the gold border too, and close buttons named <Window>ClosePanelButton get StzxUI's look.
- Options window: the sidebar groups the sections' pages into folders (Frames, Look, Gameplay, History) that open and close; one is open at a time and the folder of the page you're on opens by itself. General, Sections and Profiles stay at the top and bottom. With 22 pages the list no longer runs under the Move mode and Test frames buttons.
- Helpers: works around a Blizzard bug on Forever (BankFrame.lua:43, "bad argument #1 to 'FetchNumPurchasedBankTabs'"): the bank's tab-cost money display listens for gold changes even while the bank is closed and errors once you've used the bank. While the bank is closed StzxUI tells it to stop listening; Blizzard's own code starts it again when the bank opens. Option "Fix Blizzard's bank error" (on).
- Battlegrounds (new section, in the Helpers folder): when a battleground ends, StzxUI notes the battleground, win or loss, your killing blows, honourable kills, deaths, honour and how long it ran (the scoreboard is hidden from addons during a match; if your numbers are still hidden at the end, the result is saved and the numbers filled in once the game shows them). /stzx bg (or the button on its page) shows each battleground's win rate and averages and your last matches; /stzx bg clear forgets them.
- Skins, round two: Questie's settings and My Journey windows in StzxUI's window look, and its quest tracker's backdrop flat with a thin edge in the skin colour (Questie's own backdrop and border switches still decide whether they show). DBM's options window in StzxUI's window look, its timer bars with a thin black edge and square icons (bar texture and colours stay DBM's). Blizzard's quest tracker headers: gold art gone, a dark strip with an accent line, white and accent text. Each under Skins > Other addons / Quest tracker. Their windows are skinned the moment they first appear.
- Helpers (new section, StzxUI_Helpers folder): sell grey items and repair at merchants (guild funds first if you like; a chat line says what was sold and spent); faster looting with auto loot on; NPCs with one chat option and no quests (flight masters, bankers) open their window straight away (hold Shift to see the chat); repeating combat errors ("Not enough mana", "Out of range"...) hidden through Blizzard's own per-message switches. Each has its own switch.
- Combat (new section): a movable combat timer (keeps the fight's length for a few seconds after); a pet reminder for hunters and warlocks (missing or dead, out of combat, not while mounted, on a flight or in towns unless you ask); your group's healers' mana in a small movable box (players marked as healers, or the classes that can heal when nobody is).
- Reminders (new section): a small list out of combat when your own buffs are missing (Arcane Intellect, armours, Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Aspects, paladin auras and blessings; group versions count), food, elixirs and long buffs are running out, rogues and shamans have nothing on their weapon, gathering tracking is off, or hunters are low on ammo.
- Quests: accept offered quests and hand in finished ones automatically, off by default (switch each on under Quests > Quest helpers; hold Shift to do it yourself; never picks between reward choices, never accepts PvP-flagging quests, never pays gold, skips grey quests by default). Reward choices show their vendor price; the most valuable gets a gold coin.
- Tooltip: the skill a herb, ore vein or skinnable corpse needs, coloured by your own skill like recipes (red, orange, yellow, green, grey). Out of combat; English node names.
- Social: the "My characters" list beside Send Mail now comes from StzxUI's shared character list, only shows characters of your faction, and colours them by class.
- Info: new readouts in the info bar, each with its own switch and place (left, centre, right): Hearthstone (where it's set, or its cooldown), Gold (hover: every character's gold, the total and this session's change), Bags (free slots, yellow at 6, red at 2; hover for the reagent bag), Durability (most worn item, yellow under 50%, red under 25%; hover for each worn item). A thin XP bar along the bottom of the info bar, rested XP shown lighter (hidden at max level). Addon memory use in the stats tooltip, biggest first (not measured in fights). Each can also sit on its own in move mode.
- Core: StzxUI remembers your characters account-wide (name, class, faction, level, gold) in StzxUIDB.characters, for the gold tooltip and mailing your alts.
- Skins: Blizzard's buff and debuff bar and the Cooldown Manager (tracked buffs as icons and bars, and cooldowns) in StzxUI's look: square cropped icons with a thin edge (debuffs edged by type where the game shows it, red when hidden; weapon enchants purple), no round masks or gold rings, flat bars and swipes. These are Blizzard's own displays, so they keep working in fights. Option "Blizzard's buffs and Cooldown Manager in StzxUI's look" (on).
- Unit Frames: buff and debuff rows no longer go blank in fights. Tested on Forever: in combat the game refuses addons every unit's auras, even your own. Rows now keep the icons they had when the fight started, each dropping off when its time runs out, and are read again as soon as combat ends. A new target mid-fight gets an empty row (never the old target's auras). Party and raid debuffs work the same way.
- Fix (Skins): the flight map window was blank. Blizzard paints the map onto the window's background texture, which the skin hid as frame art; the map and its route lines are now kept.
- Fix (Skins): gossip text (the first window at a trainer or quest giver) stayed dark brown, and quest names in it and in quest-giver lists stayed black. That window colours its own text through Blizzard's "quest text contrast" setting, and quest names carry a black colour code in the text itself; StzxUI now follows each re-colour and ignores those codes, as Blizzard's own light-text mode does.
- Skins: "Dialogue text colour" (White by default, Light grey, StzxUI blue, or Blizzard's own) and "Dialogue text size" (default 15, Blizzard's is about 13; titles 3 bigger) for quest, gossip, book and letter text, with a black shadow behind it. Colour changes apply at once, including text already on screen. Replaces the light-text checkbox.
- Skins: light text on the dark windows. Blizzard writes quest, gossip, book and letter text in dark brown or black for its parchment; its dark font objects are now light (titles gold), quest text is brightened again after Blizzard's quest code colours it, and any dark text in a skinned window is brightened when it opens. Option "Light text in quest, gossip, book and letter windows".
- Fix (Skins): a panel bigger than its window (seen in the gossip window) got a box that stuck out past the window. Panels now only get a backdrop if they fit inside their window.
- Skins: full restyle (new "How much to restyle" option, Full by default). Inside skinned windows too: panels get black backgrounds with softer accent edges, bars (reputation, skills) go flat on black keeping Blizzard's colours, checkboxes become flat boxes with an accent tick, list rows lose their art, gold scroll arrows turn grey. The window's edge is now drawn above everything inside it, so panels no longer cover it. Darker look overall. The Character window's round side tabs become square icons in black boxes, the chosen one edged in the accent. Anything with an icon (items, spells, talents) or protected is never touched. "Frames only" gives the lighter restyle.
- Nameplates: objects (fallen logs, chairs, quest items to pick up) get no StzxUI plate by default, or a name only (option "Objects"). Blizzard flags them with UnitIsGameObject / widgets-only plates.
- Nameplates: "Friendly players and NPCs: just their name" (on by default): no bar, players' names in their class colour, NPCs green. A player turning hostile (PvP flag) gets the full plate.
- Unit Frames: player and target frames. Round class icon or character portrait with a level badge, health (number, percent or both), power numbers, class/reaction colours, mirrored target. Click to target, right-click for the menu, works in combat. Each has its own movable cast bar (countdown, channels drain, interrupted/failed in red).
- Unit Frames: hides Blizzard's player and target frames (option).
- Skins: Zygor Guides' window in StzxUI's colours (edge in the skin colour, StzxUI's dark background, Zygor's own opacity kept; re-applied after each of Zygor's skin passes). Best with Zygor's "Stealth" style. Option under Skins > Other addons. Also skins Forever's Social window and the legacy system window.
- Unit Frames: "Length" slider for each frame (player, target, focus 140–400 px; target-of-target and pet 80–240 px); buffs row follows. In move mode, Ctrl + mouse wheel makes a frame longer or shorter (10 px a notch); the plain mouse wheel still grows or shrinks the whole frame. Waits for combat to end (secure frames).
- Tooltip (new section, in the StzxUI_Skins folder): tooltips in StzxUI's look, with the edge in the item's quality colour or the player's class colour; slim flat health bar; text size slider (default 13, Blizzard's is 12); extra lines: who a unit is targeting (out of combat), item level on gear and item/spell IDs (both off by default); option to show the tooltip at the mouse.
- Skins (new section): Blizzard's windows in StzxUI's look. Dark background (opacity adjustable), thin edge in StzxUI's accent, your class colour or black, a darker title strip with an accent line, flat tabs (the chosen one marked in the accent), flat buttons that light up on hover, a flat close button, slim scrollbars and darker inner panels. Around 50 windows: character, spells, talents, social, quests, merchants, trainers, mail, bank, auction house, professions, group finder, PvP, collections, calendar, game menu, addon list, macros and more. Windows Blizzard loads on first open are skinned as they load; skinning waits for combat to end. Core: StzxUI.Skin (StzxUI/Skin.lua), reusable by other sections.
- Nameplates: your debuffs now show in fights. The game refuses addons a unit's auras in combat ("Auras cannot be accessed when secret while tainted"), so by default StzxUI shows Blizzard's own debuff row (Blizzard's code may read them) above the StzxUI plate, restyled square with a thin black edge and numbers sized to the icon. New "Debuff icons" option: Blizzard's in StzxUI's look (default) or StzxUI's own (out of fights only).
- Nameplates: "Name size" slider (default 14, was 10); the level grows with it and debuffs move up to stay clear.
- Fix: a second, bigger name appeared above nameplates in combat. Blizzard's shared unit-frame code sets its (hidden) plate visible again during range/status updates; StzxUI now puts it straight back to invisible.
- Auras: if the game refuses a list sorted by time left, StzxUI asks again unsorted. (Not the cause of the missing nameplate debuffs after all: in game, the game refuses the request itself in combat: "Auras cannot be accessed when secret while tainted". Being worked on.)
- `/stzx auras`: for you, your target, party1, your pet and each nameplate, how many of your debuffs the game reports, or that it refused. Run it in a fight.
- Info: info bar across the top of the screen in StzxUI's style (dark fill, accent line), holding the readouts; choose left, centre or right for each. Bar length 30–100% of the screen, movable in move mode. New "Text size" slider (default 13, was about 10) for all readouts, in the bar or on their own; it replaces mouse-wheel scaling for the readouts. Turning the bar off puts each readout back in its own movable box.
- Action Bars: "Button border" option: the 1-pixel edge round each button in black (default) or StzxUI's accent colour.
- `/stzx buttons`: lists the addon buttons gathered from the minimap, with how faded each one is and whether its icon is tinted or greyed out.
- Fix: the minimap addon-button panel had no place on screen until first opened, so gathered buttons had none either; Zygor asks where its button is at start-up and errored (NotificationCenter.lua:434). The panel is now placed from the start.
- Options: StzxUI's entry under Esc > Options > AddOns is now a welcome page (big StzxUI title, version, what it does, which sections are on) with a big "Open StzxUI options" button plus Move mode and Test frames. The options that were on that page moved to StzxUI > General.
- Minimap: addon buttons fly-out. A small square button (accent outline) in the minimap's bottom-right corner opens a panel holding every addon's minimap button: all LibDBIcon buttons, plus (optional) other addons' button-sized buttons; Blizzard's own buttons and map pins (Questie, HandyNotes) stay put. Settings: on/off, other addons' buttons, buttons per row, which side it opens, close after a click. Escape closes it; turning it off hands every button back.
- Minimap (new section): square minimap with a 1-pixel border (black or StzxUI accent), the round frame art and compass ring hidden, + and - buttons hidden (the mouse wheel already zooms). Re-applied after Blizzard's own minimap setup; minimap-button addons are told it's square. Edit Mode still places it.
- Inventory (new section): remembers the bags, gear and bank tabs of every character (and the shared account bank, each time a bank is opened). Item tooltips list who else has one ("Sorchawyn Ashvale: 12 (bags)"); `/stzx items [words]` searches every character, hover for the item. Read-only.
- Blizzard Frames (new section): the Blizzard frames Edit Mode can't place get boxes in StzxUI move mode (Blizzard group): alerts, error messages, zone text, capture bars and scores, boss banner, level-up toasts. They start exactly where Blizzard puts them; drag to move, mouse wheel to resize, right-click to put back. Loot roll frames and the widgets under the minimap are left to Blizzard's own layout.
- Movers can attach a frame by any point (Blizzard frames keep their own anchor).
- StzxUI options window: `/stzx` now opens StzxUI's own window with every page in a sidebar (General, Sections, each section, Profiles), a search box that finds options on any page, and Move mode / Test frames buttons. It writes through the same settings as Blizzard's panel (Esc > Options > AddOns > StzxUI still works and has a button into the window).
- Nameplates (new section): StzxUI's own plates on every nameplate addons may touch (Blizzard's stays, invisible, so clicking and targeting work as before). Health bar in reaction or class colour with percent, name, level (difficulty colour, + for elites), raid marker; your target outlined in StzxUI blue and other plates faded (adjustable); red edge when a mob is attacking you; cast bars yellow when you can interrupt, grey with a shield when you can't, blue for channels, "Interrupted" when stopped; your debuffs above the name. All secret-safe in combat. Test plates with `/stzx test`. Friendly players inside dungeons keep Blizzard's plates (the game forbids changing them).
- Aura icons now live in the core (shared by unit frames, party/raid and nameplates).
- Action Bars (new section): Blizzard's bars restyled, visuals only. Square cropped icons with a 1-pixel border and a dark fill for empty slots (Blizzard's rounded frame art hidden), hover/pressed/active in StzxUI colours, keybind/stack/macro/cooldown text sized to the button, short keybinds (M4, S1, N5), optional red icons out of range, options to hide keybinds or macro names. Edit Mode still places and sizes the bars and handles the gryphons.
- Test frames: pretend party (5) and raid (40) members drawn exactly like the real frames with your current settings, including dead, offline, out-of-range, leader, raid marker, target and debuff examples, so you can see and place the frames without a group. Switch with `/stzx test`, the Test frames button on the Party & Raid page, or the new Test frames switch on the move mode toolbar (those go away when move mode closes). Hidden when combat starts.
- Profiles: importing now asks what to call the new profile, suggesting the name it was shared under (exports now carry it; older strings suggest "Imported").
- Unit Frames: buff and debuff icons tidied. A 1-pixel gap between icons (new "Space between icons" slider, 0-6), borders drawn inside each icon, and the time-left and stack numbers sized to the icon so they stay inside it. Icons a touch bigger by default (24, small frames 20); saved sizes still on the old default move up too (saved data version 3).
- Fishing (new section): lure timer while you hold a fishing pole ("Lure 4:32", red in the last 30 seconds, a chime when it runs out) and a catch log per zone saved for all your characters. Hover the timer for this zone's catches with percentages; `/stzx fishing` lists them.
- Social: recent mail names. A list beside the Send Mail tab of the people you mailed last and your own characters (noted as you log in to each). Click a name to fill in the To box; right-click a recent name to forget it. Saved for the whole account.
- Runs (new section): dungeon and raid run history per character: time, XP (across level-ups), gold gained or spent, bosses killed, deaths, and green-or-better loot you picked up. A /reload or quick disconnect inside keeps the same run. Movable run timer while inside; `/stzx runs` opens the history window (hover a run for its bosses and loot). Loot rolls you win aren't counted: the game keeps loot messages secret inside instances.
- Quests: party quest status. A small panel beside the quest giver window and the quest log's detail page lists your party and whether each is on that quest (On it / Not on it / Offline). The quest log panel has Share and Share all (one quest a second); `/stzx shareall` does the same.
- Move mode works alongside Blizzard's Edit Mode: opening Edit Mode opens move mode too (and closes it again), the toolbar moves to the top-left out of Edit Mode's way, StzxUI uses Edit Mode's grid size while its grid is showing, and StzxUI frames snap to the edges of Blizzard's frames (action bars, minimap, chat...). Setting: "Open with Edit Mode".
- Info: levelling pace readout, "Lv 18  45%  12.3k xp/h  1h 05m": XP per hour this session and the time to the next level at that pace. Movable, hidden at max level; `/stzx xp` restarts the count.
- Quests (new section): quests new to WoW: Forever get a glow on the parchment (quest giver window and quest log detail page); colour, strength and height adjustable. Quest item sparkles stay on after graphics presets. `/stzx quest` tells you whether the open quest is new.
- Party & Raid (new section): StzxUI party and raid frames. Health, name, debuffs (all, or only ones you can remove), range fading, target highlight, leader crown and raid markers; left-click targets, right-click opens the menu, in combat too. Size, spacing, growth and power bar are adjustable live, and each has a mover. Blizzard's party and raid frames are hidden while StzxUI's are on.
- Unit Frames: focus frame (with its own cast bar), and small target-of-target and pet frames. Each can be switched off; Blizzard's versions are hidden where StzxUI shows its own.
- Unit Frames: buffs and debuffs on both frames: duration swipe, stack count, debuff border by type, tooltip on hover. Options per frame: show buffs, show debuffs, only my debuffs (target), icon size, above or below. Works in combat (the game filters; StzxUI only displays).
- Move mode: snap to other frames' edges and centres, mouse wheel to resize a frame (saved per profile), a Show row to hide groups of boxes.
- Accent colour option: StzxUI blue or your class colour.
- StzxUI's addons are grouped together in the AddOn list.
- Unit Frames: optional JiberishUI Icons support. With that addon installed, its class and race styles are portrait choices; NPCs fall back to their face.

### Fixed
- Characters were saved as "Name - " on Forever (the game gives no realm name). Now just "Name"; existing choices carry over.
- Core: profiles. Each character picks one; new from current, reset, delete, export and import as a text string. Your existing settings move into a "Default" profile automatically.
- Core: move mode (`/stzx move`): grid from the screen centre, snap to grid (Shift to place freely), arrow-key nudge, right-click to reset one frame, Reset all, closes in combat.
- Core: Sections switches to turn whole modules on or off per profile.
- Core: StzxUI pop-up window for names, profile strings and "reload now?".
- Info: movable FPS/latency/clock readout and map coordinates.
- Core: StzxUI options page, saved settings, `/stzx` command, module system.
- Social: decline duel requests (and duels to the death), with an optional chat notice.
- Social: thank players who buff you, by emote or whisper, out of combat only, once per player per few minutes.
